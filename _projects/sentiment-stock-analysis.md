## Project Overview
Research analyzing how public sentiment and social commentary correlate with equity price oscillations on the Colombo Stock Exchange.

## Methodology
- Scraped real-time market discussions from social platforms.
- Applied NLP tokenization and TextBlob polarity scoring.
- Trained predictive classification models in Scikit-Learn to forecast price direction.
